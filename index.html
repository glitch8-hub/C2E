A parent and his brave 10-year-old, Maya, need your help... and the only way out is through. Walk through 4 rooms of their CHEO journey. Search each scene, gather clues, crack the side puzzles, and solve each room's challenge to reveal one digit of the code. Crack all four, open the final lock, and unlock a bright, healthy future for Maya! Along the way you will cross paths with some of CHEO's 47 Health Disciplines: specialized professions, once called Allied Health, who assess, treat and support kids and families every single day. You won't meet all 47 tonight, but you will get to know quite a few.
